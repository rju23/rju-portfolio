Customer Experience

The Android App

Customers browse services, pick a location on the map, and book an appointment in three steps — all from their phone.

📋
Browse Services 14 services with per-vehicle pricing. Search, filter, and view full details.
📅
Book in 3 Steps Select service → confirm location on map → review & pay.
📍
Live Map Location Google Maps integration. Customers pin their exact address or save it for next time.
🚗
Vehicle Profiles Save multiple vehicles. Pricing auto-adjusts by vehicle type.
🔐
Auth & Accounts Email/password and Google Sign-In. Manage account, vehicles, and addresses.
🕒
Lead Time Aware Booking respects admin-set lead times. Past dates and blocked slots are greyed out.

The Admin Dashboard

A web-based control panel for the business owner. Manage bookings, services, drivers, customers, and operational settings — all in real time.

At-a-Glance Overview

Total bookings, pending, completed, and customer count — live from Firestore.

Booking Management

Search and filter bookings by status. View booking timeline and update status.

Customer Management

View all registered customers and their booking history.

Driver Management

Add and manage drivers assigned to service bookings.

All Services

View and manage all 14 services — both main services and add-ons in one place.

Main Services

Filter to main services only. Drag to reorder how they appear in the app.

Add-On Services

Manage optional add-ons customers can stack on top of any main service.

Lead Time — Overview

Set the minimum advance notice required before a booking can be placed.

Lead Time — Controls

Supports temporary overrides and scheduled changes for flexible operations.

Announcements — Templates

Pre-built message templates for common scenarios like high demand or weather delays.

Announcements — Scheduling

Set expiry times or schedule messages to appear automatically on a future date.

Blocked Periods

Block specific date and time ranges. Those slots appear greyed out in the customer app.
