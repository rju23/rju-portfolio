Customer Experience

The Android App

Customers browse services, pick a location on the map, and book an appointment in three steps — all from their phone.

📋
Browse Services 14 services with per-vehicle pricing. Search, filter, and view full details.
📅
Book in 3 Steps Select service → confirm location on map → review & pay.
📍
Live Map Location Google Maps integration. Customers pin their exact address or save it for next time.
🚗
Vehicle Profiles Save multiple vehicles. Pricing auto-adjusts by vehicle type.
🔐
Auth & Accounts Email/password and Google Sign-In. Manage account, vehicles, and addresses.
🕒
Lead Time Aware Booking respects admin-set lead times. Past dates and blocked slots are greyed out.

Business Operations

The Admin Dashboard

A web-based control panel for the business owner. Manage bookings, services, drivers, customers, and operational settings — all in real time.

At-a-Glance Overview

Total bookings, pending, completed, and customer count — live from Firestore.

Booking Management

Search and filter bookings by status. View booking timeline and update status.

Service Configuration

Add main services and add-ons with per-vehicle pricing. Drag to reorder how they appear in the app.

Lead Time Control

Set the minimum advance notice for bookings, with support for temporary and scheduled overrides.

Live Announcements

Push a banner message to the app instantly. Pre-built templates for common scenarios, with optional expiry.

Blocked Periods

Block specific date and time ranges. Customers see those slots greyed out automatically in the booking flow.
